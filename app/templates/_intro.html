What this demo shows

Ask a pharma commercial question in English. The agent generates SQL against a Postgres warehouse of public CMS data (Medicare Part D 2024 + Open Payments 2023 + NPI Registry, filtered to California).

The point of the demo is the right pane. Two memory layers — verified Q→SQL examples, and per-HCP facts — accumulate as you and your team use the agent. Cold-start failures become one-shot successes after one verification. HCP context accumulates across queries and across users.

Suggested first query (the "learning moment" demo arc):
Top 10 oncologists in California prescribing Herceptin in 2024
